How paying works

We credit a payment only after the official Tari library opens its commitment to the exact amount and a Tari-run public indexer shows it committed on the chain.We credit a payment only after our wallet sees it and a Tari-run public indexer confirms it exists on the chain. The amount is the one our wallet reads. You pay our receiving address with this invoice's reference inside it; the reference travels in the payment's encrypted memo, so the chain doesn't show which invoice was paid, or by whom. The network fee is public on the chain. The amount and who paid are not. For space you rent from us we see the amount, because we're the seller. Nobody else does.
