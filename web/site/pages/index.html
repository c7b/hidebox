Two locks. Only one of them is us.

Programmable secrets: you write the rule and our server keeps the time. The Ootle carries keys we can't read and payments whose amounts only the payer and the payee see, and it keeps the receipts.

Lock 1 · The Ootle (the part we can't read)

  • Carries a file's key to one Tari address, sealed inside a stealth payment.
  • Moves money as stealth TARI, checked against chain state.

3EXT. THE OOTLE — NIGHT3

Send a file to a Tari address.

No email, no account. Paste someone's Tari address. The file's key travels to them sealed inside a stealth TARI payment, and only their locker's keys open it.

  1. Your browser locks the file. We receive noise.
  2. Your browser seals the file's key to their address. We post a tTARI stealth payment that carries it.
  3. Your browser seals the file's key to their address and sends a tTARI stealth payment that carries it.
  4. Their mutis inbox finds the payment, opens the seal, then the file.

They need a Tari address. No wallet? A free locker makes one in seconds, no email.

Our relay posts the payment for you, so we learn the address you send to, never the key.

Once your browser's wallet holds its testnet grant (a minute or two after your locker opens), your browser builds and pays for the payment itself and we pass it to the network: we see that your locker sent a transaction, not who it was for. Before that, our relay posts it and learns the address, never the key.

The chain shows an output with a hidden amount, a hidden owner and an encrypted memo. The fee is public. Payments our relay sends all come from one wallet with the same fee, so someone watching could group them as “sent by mutis”, but not see to whom.

Sends from your browser spend a grant from our sponsor wallet, then each send's change. Anyone reading the Ootle can link your sends to each other and to our sponsor wallet, but not see to whom or how much.

Or let anyone leave you one.

Make a drop link in your locker and put it anywhere: a bio, a newsroom page, a security.txt. Anyone can leave a file there, with no account and no wallet.

  1. Their browser locks the file before it leaves.
  2. Our relay delivers its key to your locker inside a stealth payment on the Ootle.
  3. Your inbox finds it. Only your locker's keys open it.

A drop link is public by design, so its Tari address is too. We see which drop a file was left in and the sender's IP while they're connected, never the file.
