Who sees what

Four sides of every file.

  • You

    Sees
    Your files, their names, and what happened to each link.
    Never sees
    Who opened a link.
    You see how many times it opened.

  • The person you share with

    Sees
    What the rule still allows.
    Never sees
    Your other files.

  • Our server

    Sees
    A padded size, the hour it arrived, its rule, and how many times it opened each hour.
    Never sees
    The name, the type, what's inside, the key, or who opened it.

  • The Tari chain

    Sees
    That a payment happened, and its fee.
    Never sees
    The amount, who got it, or the key.

Our server sees your IP address while you're connected.
We don't write it to disk: rate limits use a keyed hash, kept in memory, with a key that changes every day.
Every detail: Under the hood.

Tari does the private parts

The chain shows a payment with a hidden amount and a hidden owner.
The fee is public.

  • Pay to open: they pay your Tari address, then it opens.
  • Send by Tari: the key rides inside a payment to their address.
    Only they can open it.
  • When a file leaves your drive, we write that on the Tari chain, for anyone to check.

What's tTARI?

  • tTARI is the coin on Tari's test network.
  • When your drive opens, our sponsor wallet sends it some tTARI to try things.
  • On a pay page, tap Pay with test money.
    Our demo wallet pays the owner for you, with test money.
