Using mutis

You may use mutis to share files and notes you have the right to share. You may not use it to:

  • share child sexual abuse material, in any form. We remove it and report it to the authorities;
  • spread malware, or host pages or files meant to steal passwords, money or identities;
  • harass, threaten or expose anyone, including by sending them deliveries they didn't ask for through their Tari address or drop link;
  • share anything illegal where you or the recipient are, or anything that infringes someone else's rights;
  • attack mutis, its users or the Tari testnet, or get around our limits with many networks, lockers or scripts.

We can't see inside files, so we rely on reports. Anyone can report a link.

A testnet service, with no warranty

mutis runs on the Esmeralda testnet of the Tari Ootle. tTARI has no monetary value, payments use play money, and the testnet can be reset. The service is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, and may change or stop. It is not a backup: keep your own copy of anything you can't lose.

Limits and space

Free space is a fair share of one real disk, recalculated every hour, and it can go down. When it does, nothing of yours is deleted, but you can't add more until you're under it again. If the disk truly fills, new uploads pause for everyone. Rented space adds to your share for the period you paid for. The live limits are on Pricing and limits

Your locker and its key

A locker is a key your browser makes; there is no email and no password with us. Your recovery code or key file is the only way back into it. If you lose it, nobody, including us, can let you back in, and your kept files are lost with it. Anyone who has it can open your locker, so keep it private.

Deletion, expiry and the end of rented space

A link leaves when its rule says so: after its opens, at its time, or when you delete it. We then delete the ciphertext within a minute (after a download in progress finishes). When rented space ends, your limit goes back to the free share; nothing is deleted for , and after that, if you're still over, your oldest kept files are removed until you fit. Files kept by a locker that hasn't signed in for are deleted.

Removal

We can remove any link, file or drop by its id, without seeing inside, when it's reported and we believe it breaks these terms, or when it's abusing limits. We usually disable first and ask questions later.

What we can hand over

If compelled by law, we can hand over what we have: ciphertext, the columns listed on our privacy page, and invoices. We can't hand over contents or keys, because we never have them.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version appears on this page and in the repository, with the date. Using mutis after a change means you accept it.
