How it works

Eight pictures. One minute.

  1. You give the ghost a secret: a note, a photo, any file.

  2. It gets locked right here, before it goes anywhere.

  3. Our server gets scrambled noise. Never the key.

  4. The key hides in the link, after the # sign.

  5. Your friend taps the link and sees it one time.

  6. Then it's gone. We delete our scrambled copy.

  7. Its goodbye is written on the Tari™ chain, for anyone to check.

  8. Or send it to a Tari address. Only they can open it.
