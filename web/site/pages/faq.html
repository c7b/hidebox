1INT. THE LOBBY — INTERMISSION1

Questions, answered short.

About mutis, built on the Tari™ Ootle testnet. The long answers, with every boundary, are on How it works

Can you see my files?

No. Your browser encrypts each file with AES-256-GCM before upload. We store ciphertext and never receive the key.

What do you actually store?

A random id, the ciphertext, a padded size, the hour it arrived, its rule and its open count, plus a few technical columns. Each link's “What we know” page prints every column, and our privacy page lists every table.

What happens when a once-only link is opened?

The recipient clicks Open, their browser decrypts the file, and our server deletes the ciphertext when the download finishes. Reloading shows “This secret already made its exit.” If you didn't open it, someone else did.

Can I get a deleted file back?

No. We keep no backups of files, and we never had the key.

Our host also takes automatic snapshots of the whole server and keeps them for a few weeks. A file that existed when one was taken stays in it, as ciphertext, until that snapshot is rotated out.

Will a link preview in WhatsApp or Slack use up my one view?

No. Opening takes a click. But the preview service now has the full link, so use Link + password or send it to one Tari address for anything sensitive.

Do I need crypto or a wallet?

No. Links, passwords, once-only, views and expiry are free and need nothing. Tari™ features use tTARI, and we give demo tTARI on the testnet.

What's Tari, and what's the Ootle?

Tari is an open-source, proof-of-work privacy blockchain. The Ootle is its layer for applications, where payments are private by default and can carry sealed messages. mutis uses it to deliver keys and move money.

Is tTARI real money?

No. It has no monetary value. Ootle mainnet launches ; paid features stay on the testnet until our code is reviewed.

Is mutis anonymous?

No account and no email. That's not anonymity: our server still sees your IP while you're connected. See what we can't protect you from.

What is an exit receipt?

Every exit goes on the public record: we add it to SealLog on the Ootle at the next checkpoint. It proves we said we deleted it, publicly and permanently. It can't prove we kept no copy.

What if I lose my key?

Your locker is gone for good. Nobody can reset it. That's the price of nobody being able to take it.

What's the safety code?

Your safety code is 12 emoji (96 bits) from your locker's public keys. Compare all 12 with the person, out loud or on a call. For a plain Tari address, compare the first and last 6 characters instead.

What does a view key show, and should I share it?

Arrivals and amounts. It can't spend, and it can't open files sealed to your locker. The exception: a sender can choose “The recipient uses another Ootle wallet”, and those files do open with the view key. Share it only with someone allowed to see what arrives.

Can someone send me files I didn't ask for?

Anyone with your Tari address or drop link can. Deliveries never open by themselves, programs get a warning, and you can close a drop link anytime.

How big can files be?

without a locker, with a free locker, with rented space.

The live numbers are on Pricing and limits.

Why does my free space change?

It's a fair share of a real disk, recalculated hourly, not a marketing number.

Can you scan files for viruses?

We can't see inside, so we can't scan it either. Only open files you expected. Programs get a warning before you can save them, and people without a locker can't send them at all.

Why should I trust your JavaScript?

SRI only pins files to this page; it can't protect you from us. What can: our verify-deploy script and running your own copy.

Cookies? Analytics?

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts. Your browser contacts a Tari-run indexer only when you press “Check it on Tari's indexer”. Your key and preferences live in your browser's own storage.

Can I use mutis to share illegal things?

No. See the terms and how to report a link. We remove reported links by id, without needing to see inside.

Is mutis official Tari?

No. It's an independent open-source project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tari Labs, the Tari Council, or the Tari project.
