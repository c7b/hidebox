For judges

Two short tracks. Each step says what you should see. If you see something else, it's a bug: tell us in our thread on the Tari™ community forum.

A computer and a phone, about two minutes

No wallet. No sign-up.

  1. ComputerOpen the home page and tap Make my drive.Your drive opens. Its key was made in this browser, with your Tari address beside it. No email.
  2. ComputerDrop a small folder on your drive.Each file says Locking, then Uploading. The folder shows up with its files inside.
  3. ComputerOpen the folder. On one file tap Share, keep Once, turn on Delete it from my drive after, then tap Make link.A link and a code to scan. It says the file leaves your drive after its one open.
  4. PhoneScan the code. Tap Open it. Then reload the page.The file opens on the phone. After the reload: “It's gone.”
  5. ComputerOpen Activity.Opened, then it left your drive. That goes on the Tari chain within .
  6. ComputerTap What our server knows.A padded size, the hour, the rule, how many times it opened each hour, and its folder. Never the name, the key or who opened it.

The Tari part, on the same two

About five minutes. Test tTARI only.

  1. ComputerTap My Tari address, then Settings.Your drive's Tari address, and a code to scan.
  2. PhoneScan that code. Tap Choose a file, pick one, then tap Send.It goes by Tari. The amount and the owner are hidden. The note is locked.
  3. ComputerOpen Received. Open what came.Your file. Only this drive's keys could find it.
  4. EitherOpen Storage and tap Get more space. Tap Pay with test money.Tap Pay from my drive.Checked on the chain, then added.
