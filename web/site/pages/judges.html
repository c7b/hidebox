Have a judge pass?

Passes come from us, on request. One skips the anti-bot check and draws on a budget we've set aside, so the demo budget is much less likely to be empty for you. It's kept in this browser for a week. A pass link (/judges#pass=…) saves it when opened: open it on both devices.

If something's off

Tari features paused? The testnet is being reset; Track A still works. A step fails twice? Wait a minute and retry once, then tell us in the forum thread with the step number.

What each step shows

  • Stealth outputs: B2, B3 and B6 move value with the amount and owner hidden.
  • Encrypted memos: B2 and B3 carry a file's key sealed to one locker; only that locker's keys open it.
  • View keys separate from files: B5 sees arrivals but opens nothing.
  • Payments checked against chain state: B6 is credited only after the official Tari library proves its amount and a Tari-run indexer shows it on the chain.B6 is credited only after a Tari-run indexer confirms the payment exists on the chain; the amount is the one our wallet reads.
  • No server-side keys: A1 to A4. Our server holds ciphertext; the key stays in the link.
