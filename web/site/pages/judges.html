Track B · 5 minutes, the Tari™ part

Two devices. The phone is the recipient; the computer is everyone else.

Do thisYou should see

  1. B1On your phone, open the home page → Open my locker. When asked to save your key, tap Later.A locker opens in a few seconds, with no email: a 12-emoji safety code and an otl_esm_… Tari address.
  2. B2On the phone: Leave me a file → Make my drop link. On the computer, open that short link and drop a photo.“Location and camera data removed”, then Delivered in about a minute. The phone's inbox shows it under Left in your drop.
  3. B3On the computer: drop a file → Send it to a Tari address instead → paste the phone's address (copy it on the phone and send it to yourself in any messenger) → Send.Sent, On the Ootle ✓ and What the chain sees: amount hidden, owner hidden, memo encrypted, fee public.
  4. B4On the phone: Inbox.A delivery that only the phone's keys could find; the scan line shows how many outputs it checked. Open it: your file.
  5. B5On the phone: See what a view key sees.Deliveries and amounts, and 0 files readable with this key. A view key sees arrivals; it can't spend and can't open the files sealed to this locker.
  6. B6On either device: Get more room → the smallest pack → Pay with demo tTARI.Waiting → Seen on chain → Amount proven → Added within about a minute. Our demo wallet pays our own invoice: a real stealth transaction with play money.
  7. B7On either device: Verify → the file from B3.An inclusion proof, a root that matches the SealLog component on the Ootle, and the exit receipt from A3.

If something's off

Tari features paused? The testnet is being reset; Track A still works. A step fails twice? Wait a minute and retry once, then tell us in the forum thread with the step number.

What each step shows

  • Stealth outputs: B2, B3 and B6 move value with the amount and owner hidden.
  • Encrypted memos: B2 and B3 carry a file's key sealed to one locker; only that locker's keys open it.
  • View keys separate from files: B5 sees arrivals but opens nothing.
  • Payments checked against chain state: B6 is credited only after the official Tari library proves its amount and a Tari-run indexer shows it on the chain.B6 is credited only after a Tari-run indexer confirms the payment exists on the chain; the amount is the one our wallet reads.
  • No server-side keys: A1 to A4. Our server holds ciphertext; the key stays in the link.
