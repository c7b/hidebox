For judges

Two tracks, six steps each. See something else? It's a bug: tell us on the Tari™ community forum.

Have a judge pass?

We give them on request. A pass skips the robot check and uses test tTARI we keep for judges. It stays in this browser for a week. Open your pass link on both devices.

1

One device, no wallet

About two minutes. No sign-up.

  1. On the home page, tap Open your drive.Your drive opens, with your Tari address. No email.
  2. On a computer, drop a small folder on your drive (under on your first day).Each file says Locking, then Uploading. The folder shows Private.
  3. On one file: Share, keep Once, turn on Delete it from my drive after, Make link.“Your link is ready.” The file leaves after its one open.
  4. Open the link in a private window. Tap Open it, then reload.It opens. After the reload: “This link has ended.”
  5. Back in your drive, open Activity.Opened, then “It left your drive.” That goes on the Tari chain within .
  6. Tap What our server knows.A padded size, the hour, the rule, how many times it opened each hour, and its folder. Never the name, the key or who opened it.
2

Two devices, with Tari

About five minutes. Test tTARI only. What's tTARI?

  1. ComputerOn a folder's menu: Share, Someone who pays you, keep the first price, Make pay link.A pay link and a code to scan.
  2. PhoneScan the code.The price and the fee. The names inside stay locked.
  3. PhoneTap Pay with test money.Waiting for your payment, Payment seen, On the chain, Ready to open.
  4. PhoneOpen one photo, then save it.It opens. Our server never had the whole key.
  5. ComputerOpen Received, then Payments.The payment, marked Yours to spend.
  6. PhoneTap What the chain sees.The amount and the owner are hidden. The fee is public.

Two more, if you have time

  • Send by Tari: scan My Tari address with the phone, then send a file. It goes by Tari. Only they can open it.
  • More space: Storage, Get more space, Pay with test moneyPay from my drive. Checked on the chain, then added.
  1. ComputerTap My Tari address, then Settings.Your drive's Tari address, and a code to scan.
  2. PhoneScan that code. Tap Choose a file, pick one, then tap Send.It goes by Tari. The amount and the owner are hidden. The note is locked.
  3. ComputerOpen Received. Open what came.Your file. Only this drive's keys could find it.
  4. EitherOpen Storage and tap Get more space. Tap Pay with test money.Tap Pay from my drive.Checked on the chain, then added.
