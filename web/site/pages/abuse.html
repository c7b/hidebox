What happens next

We read every report. We usually disable the link first and ask questions later.

For child sexual abuse material, also report it to NCMEC's CyberTipline or your national INHOPE hotline.

What we can and can't do

We can delete any link, file or drop by id. We can't see what's inside, and we keep no IP logs that would identify who uploaded it.

If you leave the key out, your report never contains it: your browser removes everything after the # before sending.

Legal requests

If compelled, we can hand over what we have: ciphertext, the columns on our privacy page, invoices. Not contents. Not keys.

Something confidential? Leave us a file in our own dead drop: it's encrypted in your browser and reaches only our locker.
